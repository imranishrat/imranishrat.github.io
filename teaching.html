Teaching & Learning

My teaching focuses on supply chain management, operations management, project management and research methods, with an emphasis on applied, collaborative and industry-relevant learning.

I bring more than 15 years of academic and professional experience in industrial engineering, operations management and supply chain management into my teaching.

Teaching Approach

My teaching emphasises problem-based learning, collaborative learning and the application of theoretical concepts to real-world organisational and supply-chain challenges.

I integrate contemporary industry issues, case studies, analytical methods and practical applications to help students develop the knowledge and skills required for professional practice and further research.
