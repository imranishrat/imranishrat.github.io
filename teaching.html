Teaching

Selected courses taught across industrial engineering, operations management, supply chain management and engineering management.

Supply Chain Management

  • AMSO800 – Sustainable Organisations

    Environmental, social and economic sustainability; Porter’s Value Chain; circular economy; Industry 4.0; ESG.

  • AMQM800 – Quantitative Methods and Contemporary Tools

    Inferential statistics; multivariate statistical analysis; correlation and regression; data wrangling; exploratory data analysis; data visualisation.

  • AMSC700 – Supply Chain and Logistics Management

    Supply chain strategy; procurement; logistics; inventory management; supply chain integration; sustainability; resilience; supply chain performance.

Operations Management

  • AMRI700 – Resource Integration Management

    Integration and management of organisational resources, with emphasis on strategic alignment, operational effectiveness and business performance.

  • AMOM632 – Operations Management

    Operations strategy; process design; capacity management; quality management; productivity; continuous improvement.

Industrial Engineering

  • IE432 – Facilities Planning

    Facilities design; flow, space and activity relationships; material handling systems; layout planning; warehouse operations; quantitative facilities planning models.

  • IE352 – Systems Analysis and Design

    System structure and requirements; system specifications; reports and interfaces; system implementation and maintenance.

  • IE311 – Operations Research I

    Linear programming; graphical solution methods; simplex algorithm; transportation and assignment problems; integer and goal programming.

  • IE255 – Engineering Management

    Management of engineering organisations; production, inventory and human resource management; project planning and control.
