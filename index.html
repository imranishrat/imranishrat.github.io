Research Focus

My research focuses on industrial engineering, supply chain management and sustainability. Drawing on an interdisciplinary background, I integrate quantitative and qualitative approaches to investigate complex real-world problems and develop analytical and context-sensitive insights for contemporary operations and supply chain challenges.

Research Themes

01

Industrial Engineering

Operations research, optimisation, quality management, process improvement and analytical decision-making.

02

Supply Chain Management

Supply chain strategy, logistics, resilience, traceability, transparency and transformative supply chains.

03

Sustainability

Sustainable and circular supply chains, responsible sourcing, social sustainability and the SDGs.
