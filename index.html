Dr. Imran Ishrat

Senior Lecturer | Operations & Supply Chain Management
Ara Institute of Canterbury, New Zealand
Incumbent Fellow, Logistics Institute of New Zealand
Affiliate, South Asia Research Institute at the Australian National University

My research focuses on transformative and sustainable supply chains, with particular emphasis on luxury and natural-fibre industries in the Global South. I integrate supply chain management, operations management and industrial engineering approaches to examine sustainability, traceability and supply chain transformation.
