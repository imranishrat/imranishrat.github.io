Research Curriculum Vitae

Research

My research sits at the intersection of supply chain management, operations management, sustainability and technological transformation. My current programme focuses particularly on transformative luxury and natural-fibre supply chains in the Global South.

Transformative Supply Chains

Understanding how supply chains can move beyond efficiency towards sustainable, equitable and resilient systems.

Sustainability & Regeneration

Examining economic, environmental, social, cultural and technological dimensions of sustainable supply chains.

Cashmere & Luxury Fibres

Investigating the transformation of cashmere and luxury natural-fibre supply chains in the Global South.

AI, Traceability & Transparency

Exploring emerging technologies for provenance, traceability, transparency and supply-chain decision-making.
