ACADEMIC PROFILE

Dr Imran Ishrat

Senior Lecturer
Supply Chain Management & Operations Management
Ara Institute of Canterbury, New Zealand

My research examines how supply chains can transform to create sustainable, resilient, transparent and equitable outcomes, with particular interest in luxury and natural-fibre supply chains in the Global South.

My work brings together supply chain management, operations management, sustainability, technological transformation and responsible governance.

Research

Cashmere & Luxury Natural Fibres

Transformation of luxury natural-fibre value chains, with particular focus on cashmere in the Global South.

AI, Traceability & Transparency

Emerging technologies, provenance, traceability and data-driven supply-chain transformation.

Transformative Cashmere Supply Chains

Challenges and opportunities in the Global South

My flagship research programme investigates how cashmere supply chains can become more sustainable, transparent, resilient and equitable while protecting cultural heritage and strengthening artisan communities.

The programme considers sustainability, technology, traceability, fair trade, governance, skills, livelihoods and market access as interconnected dimensions of supply-chain transformation.
