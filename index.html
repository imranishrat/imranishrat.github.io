ACADEMIC PROFILE

Dr Imran Ishrat

Senior Lecturer
Supply Chain Management & Operations Management
Ara Institute of Canterbury, New Zealand

My research investigates how supply chains can evolve beyond traditional efficiency driven models to deliver sustainable, transparent, and equitable outcomes, particularly within luxury and natural fibre industries in the Global South.

My research adopts a multidisciplinary perspective on transformative supply chains, integrating insights from industrial engineering, operations management, sustainability, and technological transformation in collaboration with experts from diverse fields. It explores supply chains beyond traditional measures of efficiency and profitability, examining how environmental stewardship, social wellbeing, cultural preservation, technological innovation, and participatory governance contribute to sustainable outcomes.
