About

I am a Senior Lecturer and researcher with more than 20 years of experience across Industrial Engineering, Operations Management and Supply Chain Management. My academic journey has evolved from a focus on operational efficiency and systems improvement towards research examining how supply chains can contribute to economic, social, environmental and technological transformation.

Research

My research focuses on transformative and sustainable supply chains, particularly within luxury industries, natural fibres and the Global South.

Transformative Supply Chains

Understanding how supply chains can move beyond efficiency towards positive economic, social, cultural and environmental transformation.

Sustainable Supply Chains

Investigating sustainability across economic, social, environmental, cultural and technological dimensions.

Cashmere & Luxury Fibres

Researching the challenges and opportunities surrounding cashmere supply chains, artisan communities and luxury fibre value chains.

AI & Traceability

Exploring artificial intelligence and emerging technologies for supply-chain traceability, transparency, provenance and decision-making.

Featured Research

Transformative Cashmere Supply Chains

My current research examines how cashmere supply chains in the Global South can become more sustainable, transparent, equitable and resilient. The research brings together sustainability, technology, traceability, fair trade, governance, cultural heritage and artisan wellbeing.
