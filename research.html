Research

My research examines how supply chains can move beyond traditional measures of efficiency to create more sustainable, resilient, transparent and equitable outcomes, with particular focus on luxury and natural-fibre industries in the Global South.

Research Themes

Transformative & Sustainable Supply Chains

Sustainable, resilient and responsible transformation of supply-chain systems.

Cashmere & Luxury Natural Fibres

Sustainability and transformation of luxury natural-fibre value chains in the Global South.

AI, Traceability & Transparency

Emerging technologies, provenance and data-driven supply-chain transformation.

Responsible Governance & Fair Trade

Ethical sourcing, artisan livelihoods, value distribution and responsible governance.
