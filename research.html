Research

My research focuses on improving the performance, sustainability and resilience of organisations and supply networks through the integration of industrial engineering, operations management and supply chain management perspectives.

My work is particularly concerned with how organisations can respond to technological, environmental, social and institutional change. I combine quantitative and qualitative research approaches to address complex operational and supply chain problems.

Research Themes

Industrial Engineering

Research on the design, analysis and improvement of complex systems using industrial engineering principles and analytical methods.

  • Systems optimisation
  • Quality engineering
  • Process improvement
  • Decision analysis
  • Operations research
  • Multi-criteria decision-making

Operations Management

Research examining how organisations design and manage processes, resources and operations to improve efficiency, quality, productivity and organisational performance.

  • Operations strategy
  • Process and productivity improvement
  • Quality management
  • Lean operations
  • Technology and operations
  • Operations sustainability

Supply Chain Management

Research investigating the management and transformation of supply networks, with particular attention to sustainability, resilience, transparency and emerging technologies.

  • Supply chain sustainability
  • Transformative supply chains
  • Supply chain resilience
  • Traceability and transparency
  • Emerging technologies and AI
  • Circular and regenerative supply chains

Cross-Cutting Research Areas

Across these three research themes, my research explores several interconnected areas:

Sustainable and Transformative Supply Chains

Investigating how supply chains can move beyond incremental improvements towards more sustainable, inclusive and transformative models of value creation.

AI and Emerging Technologies

Examining the application of artificial intelligence, digital technologies and advanced analytical approaches to improve supply chain visibility, decision-making and performance.

Quality and Process Improvement

Applying engineering and operations management approaches to improve quality, reliability, productivity and process performance.

Sustainability and Circular Economy

Exploring sustainable production and consumption, circular economy principles and the transition towards more environmentally and socially responsible supply networks.

Current Research Focus

A significant component of my current research examines transformative supply chains in the Global South, with particular attention to sustainable luxury and fibre supply chains.

My research on the cashmere industry investigates the interaction between sustainability, technology, governance, traceability, livelihoods and supply chain transformation. This work brings together supply chain management, sustainability and industrial engineering perspectives to understand how traditional industries can respond to changing global markets and sustainability expectations.

I am particularly interested in research that connects academic theory with practical industry challenges and generates outcomes that can contribute to more sustainable and resilient supply networks.

Research Methods

My research employs both qualitative and quantitative approaches, selected according to the nature of the research problem.

  • Case study and qualitative research
  • Semi-structured interviews and observations
  • Systematic and structured literature reviews
  • Thematic analysis
  • Multi-criteria decision-making
  • Fuzzy AHP and related decision-analysis techniques
  • Statistical analysis
  • Artificial intelligence and machine learning
  • Data analytics and computational methods
  • Quality engineering and optimisation techniques

Research Collaboration

I welcome research collaboration with academics, industry organisations, research institutes and postgraduate researchers working in industrial engineering, operations management, supply chain management and sustainability.

Areas of particular interest include sustainable supply chains, transformative supply chains, AI and emerging technologies, quality and operations improvement, circular economy and sustainable industrial systems.
