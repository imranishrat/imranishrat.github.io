RESEARCH

Transformative Supply Chains

Researching how supply chains can evolve beyond conventional measures of efficiency towards systems that are sustainable, resilient, transparent and equitable.

Research Perspective

My research sits at the intersection of supply chain management, operations management, sustainability and technological transformation.

I examine how supply chains can create value not only through operational and economic performance, but also through environmental responsibility, social wellbeing, cultural preservation, technological innovation and more inclusive forms of governance.

Core Research Question

How can supply chains transform to generate sustainable, equitable, resilient and socially meaningful outcomes while remaining economically viable?

Flagship Research Programme

Transformative Cashmere Supply Chains

Challenges and opportunities in the Global South

My flagship research programme examines the transformation of cashmere supply chains, with particular attention to artisan communities and the wider luxury natural-fibre ecosystem.

The programme explores how traditional knowledge, cultural heritage and artisan production can be integrated with contemporary sustainability, technology, governance and global market requirements.

Key Research Themes

Sustainable Production

Sustainability, circularity, responsible production and long-term supply-chain resilience.

Artisan Communities

Livelihoods, wellbeing, skills, cultural heritage and participation in global value chains.

Digital Traceability

AI, digital technologies, provenance and supply-chain visibility.

Governance & Fair Trade

Ethical sourcing, institutional arrangements, value distribution and responsible governance.

Research Methods

My research draws on interdisciplinary qualitative and quantitative approaches, including case study research, semi-structured interviews, observation, thematic analysis, multi-criteria decision-making, Analytical Hierarchy Process methods and computational approaches involving artificial intelligence and machine learning.

Transformative Cashmere Supply Chains

My flagship research programme investigates how cashmere supply chains can become more sustainable, transparent, resilient and equitable while protecting cultural heritage and strengthening artisan communities.
