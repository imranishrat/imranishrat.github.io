Research

My research is multidisciplinary, integrating supply chain management, operations management and industrial engineering to address complex organisational, technological and sustainability challenges. Drawing on sustainability, operations research, quality engineering and emerging technologies, my work examines how industries and supply chains can adapt, innovate and create social, environmental and economic value, with a growing focus on transformative and sustainable luxury and natural-fibre supply chains in the Global South.

Research Themes

Transformative & Sustainable Supply Chains

Sustainable, resilient and transformative supply chains, with particular emphasis on food, luxury and natural-fibre industries and supply chains in the Global South.

Sustainable Luxury & Natural-Fibre Industries

Sustainability, traceability, provenance, Geographical Indication and responsible production in luxury apparel and natural-fibre value chains, with particular focus on the cashmere industry.

Operations Research, Optimisation & Industrial Systems

Operations research, process optimisation, quality engineering, manufacturing systems, decision modelling and continuous improvement.

Technology, Innovation & Operational Transformation

Artificial intelligence, machine learning, emerging technologies, human factors, digital transformation, innovation and technology-enabled operational improvement.

Current Research

A significant component of my current research examines transformative supply chains in the Global South, with particular attention to sustainable luxury and fibre supply chains.

My research on the cashmere industry investigates the interaction between sustainability, technology, governance, traceability, livelihoods and supply chain transformation. This work brings together supply chain management, sustainability and industrial engineering perspectives to understand how traditional industries can respond to changing global markets and sustainability expectations.

I am particularly interested in research that connects academic theory with practical industry challenges and generates outcomes that can contribute to more sustainable and resilient supply networks.

Transformative Luxury Apparel Supply Chains and the Sustainable Development Goals

This research programme connects the transformation of luxury apparel supply chains, with particular emphasis on the cashmere industry in the Global South, to three closely aligned Sustainable Development Goals.

SDG 8 — Decent Work and Economic Growth

Promoting decent work, sustainable livelihoods and inclusive economic opportunities across luxury apparel supply chains, with particular attention to artisan communities and working conditions.

SDG 9 — Industry, Innovation and Infrastructure

Supporting innovation, technological transformation and resilient production systems within traditional luxury fibre industries, including improved supply-chain capabilities and infrastructure.

SDG 12 — Responsible Consumption and Production

Advancing responsible production, resource efficiency, circularity, traceability and sustainable consumption across luxury apparel value chains.
