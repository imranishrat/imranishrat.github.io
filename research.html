Research

Programme

  • Transformative Cashmere Supply Chains: Challenges and Opportunities in the Global South
  • AI Enabled Transformative Luxury Apparel Supply Chains

A significant component of my current research examines transformative supply chains in the Global South, with particular attention to sustainable luxury and natural-fibre supply chains.

My research on the cashmere industry investigates the interplay among sustainability, technology, governance, traceability and supply chain transformation. This work brings together supply chain management, sustainability and industrial engineering perspectives to understand how traditional industries can respond to changing global markets and evolving sustainability expectations.

I am particularly interested in research that connects academic theory with practical industry challenges and generates outcomes that contribute to more sustainable and resilient supply chains.

Transformative Luxury Apparel Supply Chains and the Sustainable Development Goals

This research programme examines the transformation of luxury apparel supply chains, with particular emphasis on the cashmere industry in the Global South, through the lens of three closely aligned Sustainable Development Goals.

SDG 8 — Decent Work and Economic Growth

Promoting decent work, sustainable livelihoods and inclusive economic opportunities across luxury apparel supply chains, with particular attention to artisan communities and working conditions.

SDG 9 — Industry, Innovation and Infrastructure

Supporting innovation, technological transformation and resilient production systems within traditional luxury fibre industries, including improved supply-chain capabilities and infrastructure.

SDG 12 — Responsible Consumption and Production

Advancing responsible production, resource efficiency, circularity, traceability and sustainable consumption across luxury apparel value chains.
