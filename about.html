About

I am a Senior Lecturer in Supply Chain Management and Operations Management at Ara Institute of Canterbury, New Zealand. My teaching and research centers around industrial engineering, operations management and supply chain management. I bring together perspectives from supply chain management, operations management, sustainability, technological transformation and responsible governance, with particular interest in luxury and natural-fibre industries in the Global South.

My current research centres on transformative supply chains, sustainable luxury and natural-fibre value chains, cashmere supply chains, AI-enabled traceability and transparency, and responsible governance. I am particularly interested in research that connects technological and environmental transformation with social and cultural value in supply chains. My work has involved academic, industry and research engagement. I am also engaged in teaching and postgraduate research supervision across supply chain management, operations management and related areas. My broader academic interests reflect a commitment to developing research that is both theoretically meaningful and practically relevant to organisations, communities and supply chains undergoing significant transformation.
