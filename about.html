About

I am a Senior Lecturer in Supply Chain Management and Operations Management at Ara Institute of Canterbury, New Zealand. I received PhD in Supply Chain Management from Massey University (New Zealand), MEng in Engineering Management from University of Ottawa (Canada), and MSc in Operations Research from Aligarh Muslim University (India).

I teach Operations Management, Supply Chain Management, and Industrial Engineering courses across Business and Engineering programs. I bring together perspectives from these areas to examine sustainable and technological transformation, with particular interest in luxury and natural-fibre industries in the Global South. My current work centres on transformative supply chains and explores how technological transformation and sustainability can create social, cultural and economic value. Broadly, I am interested in research that is relevant to organisations and communities undergoing significant transformation.

I supervise postgraduate research across supply chain management, operations management and related areas.
