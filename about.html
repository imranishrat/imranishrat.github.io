About

Dr Imran Ishrat is a Senior Lecturer in Supply Chain Management and Operations Management at Ara Institute of Canterbury, New Zealand, with over 20 years of academic and professional experience across Supply Chain Management, Operations Management and Industrial Engineering. He holds a PhD in Supply Chain Management from Massey University, an MEng in Engineering Management from the University of Ottawa, and an MSc in Operations Research from Aligarh Muslim University.

His research focuses on transformative and sustainable supply chains, with particular interest in luxury and natural-fibre industries in the Global South. His work examines how technological transformation, sustainability, traceability and responsible production can create social, cultural, environmental and economic value, with a particular focus on the cashmere industry.

His broader research interests include supply chain resilience, circular economy, operations management, industrial engineering, quality improvement, artificial intelligence and emerging technologies. His research has been presented internationally and published in peer-reviewed journals, with his doctoral research recognised through an Exceptional Doctoral Thesis distinction at Massey University.

He is an Incumbent Fellow of the Logistics Institute of New Zealand (LINZ) and an Affiliate of the South Asia Research Institute (SARI) at the Australian National University. He teaches and supervises postgraduate research across supply chain management, operations management, industrial engineering and related interdisciplinary fields.
