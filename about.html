About

Dr Imran Ishrat is a Senior Lecturer in Operations and Supply Chain Management at Ara Institute of Canterbury, New Zealand. He holds a PhD in Supply Chain Management from Massey University, MEng in Engineering Management from the University of Ottawa, and MSc in Operations Research from Aligarh Muslim University.

His research centres on transformative and sustainable supply chains, with particular emphasis on luxury and natural-fibre industries in the Global South. His work examines how technological transformation, sustainability, traceability and responsible production can contribute to social, cultural, environmental and economic value, with a particular focus on the transformation of the cashmere industry.

His broader research interests encompass supply chain resilience, circular economy, operations management, industrial engineering, quality improvement, artificial intelligence and emerging technologies. His research has been presented at international conferences and academic institutions and published in peer-reviewed journals. His doctoral research was recognised with an Exceptional Doctoral Thesis distinction at Massey University.

Dr Ishrat has extensive tertiary teaching experience in New Zealand and Asia, and teaches across Supply Chain Management, Operations Management and Industrial Engineering. He supervises postgraduate research and has published an academic book and peer-reviewed journal articles, while actively contributing to international scholarly conferences and research forums.

His scholarly contributions have received international recognition, including an Excellent Paper Presentation Award at the International Conference on Industrial Technology and Management (ICITM), University of Cambridge, UK. His academic engagement reflects a commitment to connecting rigorous research with contemporary challenges faced by organisations, industries and communities.

He is an Incumbent Fellow of the Logistics Institute of New Zealand (LINZ) and an Affiliate of the South Asia Research Institute (SARI) at the Australian National University.
