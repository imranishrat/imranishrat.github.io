About

I am a Senior Lecturer in Supply Chain Management and Operations Management at Ara Institute of Canterbury, New Zealand, with more than 20 years of professional and academic experience spanning industrial engineering, operations management and supply chain management. My academic work focuses on how organisations and supply chains can move beyond conventional measures of efficiency to create sustainable, resilient, transparent and equitable outcomes. I bring together perspectives from supply chain management, operations management, sustainability, technological transformation and responsible governance, with particular interest in luxury and natural-fibre industries in the Global South. My doctoral research examined the effects of industry transformation on quality and sustainability in luxury apparel value chains, with a particular focus on the cashmere industry in India.

My current research centres on transformative supply chains, sustainable luxury and natural-fibre value chains, cashmere supply chains, AI-enabled traceability and transparency, and responsible governance. I am particularly interested in research that connects technological and environmental transformation with social and cultural value, artisan livelihoods and responsible value creation. My work has involved academic, industry and international research engagement, including collaboration and research activities involving sustainable fibre supply chains and South Asia. I am also engaged in teaching and postgraduate research supervision across supply chain management, operations management and related areas. My broader academic interests reflect a commitment to developing research that is both theoretically meaningful and practically relevant to organisations, communities and supply chains undergoing significant transformation.
