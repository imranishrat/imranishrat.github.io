Bio

Dr. Imran Ishrat is a Senior Lecturer in Operations and Supply Chain Management at Ara Institute of Canterbury, New Zealand. He holds a PhD in Supply Chain Management from Massey University, an MEng in Engineering Management from the University of Ottawa, and an MSc in Operations Research from Aligarh Muslim University.

Imran’s current research centres on transformative and sustainable supply chains, with particular emphasis on luxury apparel and natural-fibre industries in the Global South. Guided by the United Nations 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development, his empirical research addresses themes aligned with SDG 8 (Decent Work and Economic Growth), SDG 9 (Industry, Innovation and Infrastructure), and SDG 12 (Responsible Consumption and Production).

His broader research interests include process optimisation and aviation operations management. He has authored an academic book published by CRC Press, published research in peer-reviewed journals, and presented his work at international conferences. His doctoral research was recognised with an Exceptional Doctoral Thesis Award at Massey University, and he received an Excellent Paper Presentation Award at the International Conference on Industrial Technology and Management (ICITM) at the University of Cambridge, UK.

Imran supervises postgraduate research and actively contributes to scholarly activities, including peer review for international journals such as Sustainability. He has extensive tertiary teaching experience in New Zealand and Asia and teaches across Supply Chain Management, Operations Management and Industrial Engineering.

Imran is an Incumbent Fellow of the Logistics Institute of New Zealand (LINZ) and an Affiliate of the South Asia Research Institute (SARI) at the Australian National University.
