About

I am a Senior Lecturer in Supply Chain Management and Operations Management at Ara Institute of Canterbury, New Zealand. My teaching and research centers around industrial engineering, operations management and supply chain management.

I bring together perspectives from these areas to examine sustainable and technological transformation, with particular interest in luxury and natural-fibre industries in the Global South. My current work centres on transformative supply chains and explores how technological transformation and sustainability can create social, cultural and economic value. More broadly, I am interested in research that is relevant to organisations and communities undergoing significant transformation.

I am also engaged in teaching and postgraduate research supervision across supply chain management, operations management and related areas.
