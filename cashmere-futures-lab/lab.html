CASHMERE FUTURES LAB

Transforming luxury fibre supply chains for sustainable, transparent and equitable futures.

The Cashmere Futures Lab is a research initiative exploring how cashmere and luxury fibre supply chains can respond to environmental, technological, social and governance challenges, with particular attention to the Global South.

WHY CASHMERE?

A small fibre with big supply-chain questions.

Cashmere connects pastoral communities and artisans in the Global South with international luxury markets. Its supply chain brings together questions of environmental resilience, livelihoods, technology, traceability, governance, responsible production and value distribution.

The Lab uses cashmere as a focused empirical setting through which to investigate broader questions about the transformation of luxury and global supply chains.
