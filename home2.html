Research Focus

My research focuses on industrial engineering, supply chain management and sustainability. Drawing on an interdisciplinary background, I integrate quantitative and qualitative approaches to investigate complex real-world problems and develop analytical and context-sensitive insights for contemporary operations and supply chain challenges.
